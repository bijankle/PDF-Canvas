How Smart Canvas works

Steps

  1. Drop, paste (Ctrl+V) or use Import PDF / Image to bring artwork onto a page. Each lands as a floating image with a border.
  2. Imported images are unlocked canvases. Drag one into a grid cell to snap and lock it; hold Shift to move a locked canvas. Right click an image to toggle Canvas on or off.
  3. Pick a tool on the left: V select, P pen, L polyline, A arrow, R rectangle, E ellipse, W text, Q callout, X erase. Press Esc to return to select / move.
  4. While drawing a polyline press H to make following segments faded (hidden line), press again for solid.
  5. Set colour and weight in the floating palette before drawing, or select any object and change them to restyle it.
  6. Add pages with the + below the page or in the Pages sidebar. Drag thumbnails to reorder; right click them to duplicate, copy or paste.
  7. Use Flatten to PDF to export every page, or Save Workspace to keep an editable file.

Good to know

  • Markups and images that sit fully inside a backdrop stick to it: unlock the canvas, then move or scale it and they follow. Anything hanging over an edge stays put.
  • Line weight is a fixed fraction of the canvas, so zooming never changes thickness. The Annotation scale slider, bottom right, resizes every markup at once.
  • Scroll to move up and down the pages. Hold Ctrl and scroll to zoom. Space or the middle button pans freely.
  • Text and callouts always sit in front of lines. Callout leaders connect to the nearest edge of the box and update live.
  • Ctrl+drag duplicates the item under the cursor. Double click a polyline node to remove it, double click a segment to add one.
