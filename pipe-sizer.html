How it works. Flow, velocity and diameter are linked by Q = v · (π/4) · ID². Enter any two and the third is computed; click an output badge to choose which field is solved. Diameter results use the internal diameter for the chosen size and rating.
Steel ID = OD − 2 × wall thickness (ASME B36.10M). PE100 uses the mean bore from AS/NZS 4130; the PN rating maps to the SDR series (e.g. PN10 = SDR17, PN16 = SDR11). When solving for diameter it rounds up to the next discrete size whose bore meets the required area, so the actual velocity stays at or below your target. Velocity dot: green ≈ 1–3 m/s (typical carrying range), amber outside it.
